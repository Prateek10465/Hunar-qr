    How Hunar-QR works

    One chat on a phone the learner already has. Two engines behind it: one proves skills, the other blocks fake offers.

    Dual-engine core

    Rural learner or master craftsman, on WhatsApp: voice note, work photo or clip, forwarded links and UPI QR codes.
    ↓

    Module 1: AI Skill Credentialer

    • Bhashini ASR transcribes the mentor's voice note and translates it.
    • Computer vision verifies live face and workpiece presence, anchoring the physical craft to the verified identity.
    • Practical hours map to NCrF credit (30 notional hours = 1 credit).

    Module 2: Anti-Phishing Job Shield

    • Link checks: shorteners, look-alike .gov.in names, APK files, odd endings.
    • UPI parser: collect requests, fee wording, personal IDs using government names.
    • Reply in the learner's language, then route to zero-fee listings.
    ↓
    Signed Skill Passport. W3C Verifiable Credential in a QR code, linked to APAAR or Skill India Digital Hub.
    Voice warning and safe listings. Verified openings from SIDH and ONEST that match the learner's skill tags.

    Pilot and 12-month plan

    • October 2026VIT Chennai pilot with 15 to 20 campus technicians and artisans near Kelambakkam and Vandalur, through the UBA and NSS network.
    • Months 1 to 35 rural government high schools and 2 Government ITIs in Chengalpattu and Kanchipuram districts. 500+ credentials issued.
    • Months 4 to 6Integrate the credential schema with DigiLocker, APAAR and ONEST, with guidance from IISc Bengaluru.
    • Months 7 to 12Partner with Naan Mudhalvan (TNSDC) and District Cyber Crime Cells. Reach 1,000+ rural blocks.

    Judging criteria

    Each SFIC criterion, and where to check it in this prototype.

    Clarity of the problem

    Rural learners face two traps: skills nobody can verify, and fake offers that ask for money. The demo opens with sourced figures for both.

    Originality

    Skill proof and fraud protection in one chat. The bot that mints a signed passport also reads forwarded QR codes and answers in Tamil, Hindi or English.

    Feasibility

    Signing, QR codes, QR reading and the fraud rules run in this page. Transcription, vision scoring and listings are marked as sample, with the production service named for each.

    Cost

    ₹1.40 per verified passport, from the proposal's cost table. Move the slider below to see the bill at your chosen scale.

    Beneficiary impact

    School students on bagless-day internships and apprentices get proof of skill an employer can check in seconds. Families get a place to test an offer before paying.

    Scalability

    Built on WhatsApp, Bhashini and the DigiLocker, APAAR and ONEST rails. The plan goes from a campus pilot to 1,000+ rural blocks in 12 months.
